Two presidents in the room: the Korea–Finland Startup Summit

In June 2019, President Moon Jae-in's state visit to Finland brought a delegation representing 118 Korean business entities to Helsinki. SansOx was in the room — and Korea became one of the markets where our technology gained real recognition.

Presidents Sauli Niinistö and Moon Jae-in touring the summit. Photo from the SansOx archive.

The Korea–Finland Startup Summit was held in Helsinki on June 11, 2019, during the state visit of President Moon Jae-in. Both presidents gave keynote addresses: President Niinistö told the audience that startups "play a central role for the renewal and future competitiveness of both Korean and Finnish economies."

The summit was hosted by Business Finland together with Korea's Ministry of SMEs and Startups, Korea Startup Forum, KISED, KOTRA and the Korea Chamber of Commerce & Industry. Among the agreements signed during the visit was a plan to set up a Korean startup centre in Finland.

What it meant for SansOx

For SansOx, the summit was part of a longer arc: our technology gained recognition in Korea, and we initiated a joint venture there. Then the Covid-19 pandemic halted travel and disrupted development across several regions — Korea, India and China among them.

The door that opened in 2019 has not closed. The same dissolution technology that drew attention then is now published, measured and in use — from Kuopio's wastewater plant to lake rejuvenation in India.
