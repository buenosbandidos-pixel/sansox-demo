SansOx participated in SFP-25 by Sweden in Bangladesh and Pakistan

The textile industry in Bangladesh and Pakistan is under pressure to reach zero liquid discharge. SansOx brought its water treatment to the table in Dhaka.

The textile industry plays an extremely important role in both Bangladesh and Pakistan. Swedish brands such as H&M, IKEA and Lindex manufacture a large share of their products in these two countries — and they want that manufacturing done ethically and with as little effect on nature as possible.

SFP is the answer to this. The event, organised by Business Sweden and the Embassy of Sweden in Dhaka, brings Nordic high-tech companies together to offer new ways of making textiles with little impact on nature. At the moment renewable energy is the only solution in place in most factories, at least in Bangladesh.

Water is the next constraint

SansOx brings water-improving technology to the same table, because the target is to recycle as much water as possible. Many textile companies still use groundwater as their main source, but that is changing: the Bangladesh government and European textile buyers are both pushing towards zero liquid discharge.

The Dhaka event produced good discussions, and SansOx is bringing its newest solution to the Bangladesh market first. It has been developed together with our Bangladeshi partner and helps manufacturers reach the zero-liquid-discharge target.

Pekka Bäck now takes the torch onward to the Pakistani market, where the textile industry faces much the same challenges as Bangladesh.
