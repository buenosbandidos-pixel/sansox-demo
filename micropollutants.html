Micropollutants · municipal and industrial wastewater

Removal measured in seconds, not stages.

Pharmaceutical residues, chemicals and PFAS precursors leave a treatment plant because the process was never designed to remove them. PFAS are fluorinated compounds that do not break down on their own, and ozone does not break the carbon-fluorine bond that makes them persistent — this process is not a PFAS destruction method. Whatever the process does not remove goes into the receiving water, and the compounds that do not break down stay there — which is why the 2024 directive makes this treatment mandatory rather than optional. OxTube adds the missing step inside the existing pipe.

The scale this is answering to

Two figures are cited repeatedly in Finnish reporting on this problem. Neither is a measurement we made, and neither is presented as one — they are here because they are the reason the regulation above exists at all.

92%of urban wastewater micropollutants attributed to pharmaceuticals and cosmetics, as reported by Helsingin Sanomat, 11 March 2026
1.8M kgpharmaceutical residues estimated to reach the Baltic Sea annually, as reported by Bank of Åland magazine, 2024

Both figures are cited from named media reporting, not from SansOx measurement or from a primary study we have read. They are stated here as reported, not verified by us, and should be checked against the original source before use in a regulatory or funding submission.

How the step works

Feed

Air, oxygen, ozone or a selected reagent enters the flowing water.

Dissolve and mix

OxTube increases gas contact and accelerates the target reaction.

Treat

Target compounds are broken down; unwanted gases are removed.

Verify

Sensors and sampling confirm water quality and operating performance.

Bring one compound list. We design the pilot around it.

A site pilot defines the dose, reagent, contact conditions and verification points before any performance commitment is made.
