SansOx presented the latest lake rejuvenation project in India

The Sadpura lake project was presented to the Commerce Committee of the Finnish Parliament as an example of what companies from different countries can do together.

SansOx had the honour of presenting its latest lake rejuvenation work to the Commerce Committee of the Finnish Parliament. The Sadpura lake treatment project is a joint effort between the Indo-Nordic Water Forum, the Finnish Embassy in India and Elixiir Biotech. The lake now gives pleasure to the people of Sadpura village in Haryana.

How the lake was treated

The treatment began with clearing the lake and its surroundings of rubbish. Aeration followed, supported by an enzyme treatment that cleaned the bottom of the lake. The aeration keeps working afterwards, so the dissolved oxygen in the water stays at a good level. At the same time the sewage water is treated, which is what keeps the lake healthy.

As the discussion noted, this is a good example of how companies from different countries can make a real difference together. A concrete project that produces results was a recurring theme in the conversation.
