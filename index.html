Dissolving gas into water in under a second.

Dirty water lacks oxygen; clean water needs it. OxTube is a sealed pipe section that puts oxygen or ozone into flowing water and cleans it as it passes — inside the pipes you already have. No tanks, no moving parts, no extra energy.
