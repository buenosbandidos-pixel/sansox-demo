SansOx in the North Karelian newspaper Karjalainen

Over 100 SansOx installations treat water in about 20 countries. The regional newspaper Karjalainen wrote about how the journey started and where it is going.

The North Karelian regional newspaper Karjalainen published an article about SansOx and our journey in the water business around the world.

Our aim is to improve water quality at every stage of the natural water cycle. Today SansOx offers an easy and fast way of adding gases — air, oxygen, ozone and others — as well as water treatment chemicals into water, and complete solutions built on that. There are over 100 SansOx installations treating water in about 20 countries.

From fish farms to drinking water

The journey began with solutions for cleaning water in fisheries. We now work on potable water, wastewater and natural water bodies in Asia, the Middle East and Europe.

Our deliveries include solutions for removing dissolved gases and other substances from water. Pharmaceutical residues are removed with OxTube, the basis of our technology, and with OxTube you deal with radon as well. Improving and rejuvenating natural ponds, lakes and rivers is high on our agenda too. Even in Finland, where lakes and ponds are usually in good shape, lakes are starting to appear with 0 mg/l of dissolved oxygen. Our solution, LakeOx, can improve that situation significantly and bring the water in the lake or pond back to life.

The bigger ambition

Our big dream is to provide solutions for potable water and make tap water drinkable everywhere in the world. We cannot make new water — but we can always improve the quality of what there is.
