SansOx joins forces with partners in the Bangladesh textile market

SansOx, Charm and Colormatch signed a memorandum of understanding on a joint venture to remove colour residues from textile wastewater and cut electricity use at effluent treatment plants.

SansOx entered the Bangladesh textile market to develop a way of removing colour residues from textile wastewater and to make zero liquid discharge possible — the target the Bangladeshi textile industry has set for itself.

We joined forces with two local companies, Charm and Colormatch, to develop the finished product. It combines SansOx OxTubes with automation and pumping from Charm and Colormatch. The first version was shown at DTG 2025 and drew a great deal of attention, because the results were extraordinary.

From a demonstration to a joint venture

These companies have now taken a step further, and we have signed a memorandum of understanding on founding a joint venture. The purpose is to improve water quality at effluent treatment plants and at the same time reduce electricity consumption. The first projects have already been tested, and the results range from good to excellent.

The next step is to move from the present scale to flows of tens of cubic metres per hour and on to 1,000 m³/h. The short-term target is zero liquid discharge still this year.

If you would like more information, contact Charm or Colormatch in Bangladesh, or SansOx elsewhere in the world.
