Technology — OxTube

Four functions, one tube, 0.7 seconds.

Inside the hermetic tube the water stays a controlled mixture of water, drops and gas — molecular meeting probability is high, so reactions complete immediately. The module retrofits into existing pipework and is easily removed and cleaned.

The product, in service

OxTube installed vertically at a Finnish treatment plant — stainless steel, flanged modules, instrumentation alongside. The narrowing you see is the nozzle zone where gas meets water.

The modular assembly. Flanged sections bolt into existing pipework in any order the process needs — and unbolt as easily for cleaning. Sensor ports along the line.
