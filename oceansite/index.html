Carbon credits · restoration credits · measured at the sediment

Most credits are bought on a model. Ours start at the bottom of the lake.

A degraded water body emits. Restore oxygen to the sediment and it stops — and that change can be measured at the place it happens, not inferred from a spreadsheet. OceanSite turns that measured change into two instruments: a carbon credit and a restoration credit.

Two instruments, one intervention

Climate

Carbon credits

Anoxic sediment produces methane. Oxygenating the deep zone suppresses the conditions that generate it. The avoided emission is the creditable quantity.

Nature

Restoration credits

The same intervention produces a documented ecological recovery — the unit that nature- restoration and biodiversity frameworks are built to buy.

Why they come from one project: the measurement that proves the carbon claim is the same measurement that proves the restoration claim. One baseline, one monitoring programme, two instruments. Nothing is measured twice and nothing is claimed twice.

Three things, in this order

Baseline

Before anything is installed, the deep zone is measured. Without a documented starting point there is no creditable change — only an improvement nobody can price.

Oxygenation

In-pipe OxTube oxygenation delivers gas at depth. No rotating parts in the water; operable under ice when designed for the site.

Verification

Continuous and sampled monitoring against the baseline, reported by a party that is not the supplier. A credit signed off by the vendor is not a credit.

The order is the whole business. Most restoration projects install first and measure afterwards. That produces a story. A story cannot be issued, audited or sold.

What this is and is not. Rekilampi is three years of oxygen data. It is not three years of quantified carbon abatement, and we do not present it as such. It is the evidence that the intervention does what it claims at the depth where it matters — which is the precondition for crediting, not the credit itself.

Who this is for

One group, one chain of evidence

A credit is only worth what the measurement behind it is worth, and a measurement is only worth what the treatment it describes actually did. These three steps are normally bought from three suppliers, and the evidence is handed over twice — each handover is where a buyer’s technical advisor starts asking questions. Here the three companies have the same owners.

Treat

SansOx Oy supplies the hardware in the flow: OxTube dissolving units, piping and pumps. This is the step that changes the water.

Measure

SpringNet Solutions Oy supplies the control software, the sensors and the continuous data. It records what changed, and it keeps the record.

Certify

OceanSite Oy — this company — turns a documented change into an instrument a buyer can hold: a carbon credit or a restoration credit.

What this chain does not yet deliver. Issuing a credit requires an approved methodology and a registry to issue it into. Neither has been selected. Until they are, the chain delivers treatment and the evidence of it — not issued credits. This is stated here rather than discovered by the buyer in the first technical meeting.

The three companies are separately registered. The group has no published parent company or joint trading name yet, and none is claimed on this page.
