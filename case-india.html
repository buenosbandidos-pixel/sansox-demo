Natural water · Lake rejuvenation · Haryana, India

A dead village pond, brought back — and kept clean under contract.

In Sukhrali, Gurugram — Haryana, India — the pond had become a dump for untreated inflow: covered in algae, banks eroded. With our Indian partner Elixiir, SansOx turned it into the pilot of a lake-rejuvenation programme now expanding to Sadpura village.

1The starting point

This is what the pond looked like: full algae cover, untreated water flowing in, litter on the banks. Mid-1990s Indian rivers were relatively healthy — the deterioration since has been rapid.

Before. Sukhrali pond under full algae cover. Unretouched.

2What was installed

First the untreated inflow was stopped and the banks cleaned. Then water was pumped through OxTubes to dissolve air and raise dissolved oxygen, while a natural enzyme treatment floated bottom solids to the surface for collection. Continuous OxTube aeration keeps the water clear.

The delegation with the flag of Finland at the pond — and the unveiling of the project plaque. Institutions do not pose next to projects they doubt.

Institutional record: revival executed by MICADA under supervision of the Embassy of Finland, funded through the Indo-Nordic Water Forum; the Municipal Corporation of Gurugram now runs upkeep campaigns; the project was presented to the Commerce Committee of the Finnish Parliament.

Why this matters: four dispatches across two years is the maintenance contract, visible. A revived pond that stays revived is the claim — the timeline is its receipt.

4What follows from this

The programme is expanding to further lakes in Haryana — and the mechanism scales: the same aeration that revives a village pond is what our Nature Rejuvenation mission proposes for eutrophied waters from India to the Baltic Sea.
