STP tour continues to Pakistan after Bangladesh

SansOx took part in the first Sustainable Textile Platform matchmaking event in Karachi, connecting Nordic greentech with Pakistani textile manufacturers.

SansOx was proud to take part in the first matchmaking event of the Sustainable Textile Platform in Karachi. For us it was an important step in showing how water treatment technology can make a real difference to the textile industry's green transition. With our patented oxygenation and oxidation solutions we help textile manufacturers cut chemical use, recycle process water and improve their sustainability reporting.

The event connected leading Nordic greentech providers with Pakistani textile companies producing for global brands such as IKEA and H&M. Together we looked at new ways to tackle energy, water and efficiency challenges — and turn them into openings for sustainable growth.

Warm thanks to the Embassy of Pakistan to Sweden and Finland and to Business Sweden for creating this platform. We look forward to the next steps in building partnerships that benefit both the industry and the environment.
