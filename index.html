At the heart of what we do — OxTube

OxTube is a flanged length of pipe. Water flows through it, gas is fed in from the side, and the shape of the tube forces the two to meet — no stirrer, no basin. Unbolt it to clean it. It can be installed vertically, and it goes into the pipework a plant already has. OxTube brings 5–10 times more air into the water than the flow could carry by itself; visible bubbles are simply the surplus after the saturation point has been reached.

OxTube in service at a Finnish plant. Studies and pilot cases have shown that OxTube oxygenates water more rapidly and with less energy than existing systems. Read the published article — 90 % pharmaceutical removal in 0.7 s
