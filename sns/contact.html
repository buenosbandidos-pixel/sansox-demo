Scoping

Three things, and we can size it.

Most monitoring projects start from the equipment and work backwards to the question. It is faster the other way round.

What we need from you

1. The parameter and the limit

What has to be measured, and what value has to be met or demonstrated. If there is a permit condition, the wording of it.

2. The site

Water body or installation, depth, access, and whether there is power and a communications route. Photographs help more than descriptions.

3. Who reads the report

A regulator, an auditor, a credit buyer and a board each need a different output from the same data. Knowing which one decides how the system is built.

What you get back: a proposed parameter set, an installation outline and an indication of what can and cannot be demonstrated with it — before any equipment is quoted.
