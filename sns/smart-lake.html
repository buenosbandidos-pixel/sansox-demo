Smart Lake · intelligent water quality management

A lake sampled twice a year is a lake nobody is watching.

Deep-zone oxygen collapses in weeks and recovers in weeks. Two grab samples a year cannot see that, and cannot support a permit, a restoration claim or a credit. Smart Lake sits on the bottom and measures continuously — six sensors in one hardened frame, built to stay there.

Why it is built this way

Low profile

Maximum height 350 mm. A unit that sits low on the bottom is less exposed to ice, nets and boat traffic than a floating or moored installation.

Open frame

The structure is open so water moves through it. A sensor in a sheltered enclosure measures the enclosure, not the water body.

316L stainless steel

Corrosion-resistant grade, chosen for permanent submerged installation rather than for a measurement campaign.

3 + 3 configuration

Sensors in two groups of three, so a unit can be serviced or a sensor swapped without lifting and recommissioning the whole frame.

Cabling into conduit

Sensor cables are routed to a bottom conduit — HDPE, recommended Ø 50 mm — instead of running loose across the bed.

Spare capacity from day one

Cable runs are dimensioned three in use plus two spare, so an added parameter later does not mean a new cable pull.

In the field

Who it is for

Water-area owners and municipalities

Water bodies with odour complaints, fish kills or a restoration obligation attached to a permit — where the question is whether anything is actually improving.

Restoration projects

Where an intervention has been made and its effect has to be shown over years. Continuous measurement is what distinguishes a restoration from an installation.

Crediting projects

Where the measured change becomes an issuable unit, the monitoring record is the asset. Credits are handled by OceanSite Oy; the measurement comes from here.

The order that matters: baseline, then intervention, then monitoring. Start in any other order and there is nothing to compare against — the improvement may be real and still be unprovable.

Operating specification. IP68, maximum pressure 5 bar (about 50 m depth), operating voltage 4.8–24 V. Communications: Modbus RS-485 (SDI-12 optional).
