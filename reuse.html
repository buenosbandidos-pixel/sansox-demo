Reclaimed water · agricultural irrigation

The class limit is the deliverable.

A farmer does not buy dissolved oxygen. A comunidad de regantes does not buy a pipe. They buy the right to use reclaimed water — and that right is a number in a regulation.

What decides the purchase

Spain adopted Royal Decree 1085/2024 on reclaimed water in 2024. A reuse project requires defined responsibilities, monitoring, and a declared point of compliance. The water either meets the class or it may not be used for that crop.

ClassE. coli / 100 mlPermitted use
A≤10Raw food crops with direct contact
B≤100Selected food and non-food crops
C≤1,000Drip irrigation, or no contact with the edible part
D≤10,000Industrial, energy and seed crops

Sources. Regulation (EU) 2020/741 on minimum requirements for water reuse — eur-lex.europa.eu · Directive (EU) 2024/3019 on urban wastewater treatment — eur-lex.europa.eu · Spain, Real Decreto 1085/2024 — boe.es. Regulatory summary on this site is not legal advice.

Why this shortens the sales cycle: a microbiological class limit is verified in weeks. A yield claim takes a growing season. The pilot therefore reports against the class, not against the harvest.

Why this is worth more than our own figure. E. coli is exactly what Classes A to D are counted in. A supplier reporting its own removal is normal; a university reporting it is the version that survives a permit review. Note the wording: 100 % was reached in most test runs, not all — that is how the source states it, and it is how we state it.
What is missing: a reclaimed-water reference installation. The results above come from disinfection testing and from a treated-discharge pilot, not from a reuse scheme delivering to an irrigation network under RD 1085/2024. No such installation exists yet, here or elsewhere. The first one will be a pilot, with the class as the deliverable and the measuring party agreed before the first sample.
