News from Sukhrali

Gurugram's municipal corporation launched a 30-day campaign to clean the Sukhrali village pond — the same pond that was revived using OxTubes and enzymes.

The Municipal Corporation of Gurugram has launched a 30-day campaign to clean the Sukhrali village pond and its surroundings. The aim is to conserve the water bodies within the corporation's limits.

The campaign coincided with World Wetlands Day on 2 February and its theme for the year: protecting wetlands for our common future. On the first day a range of people took part in cleaning the area around the pond, among them the corporation's brand ambassador for the Swachh Bharat Mission and representatives of several NGOs.

How the pond was revived

The Sukhrali pond was recently revived by the Micro Irrigation and Command Area Development Authority under the supervision of the Embassy of Finland, using funding from the Indo-Nordic Water Forum. The project combined two technologies: OxTubes to raise the dissolved oxygen in the water, and enzymes to clean it. The municipal corporation now maintains the project to keep the water body clean.

The campaign underlines how much wetland conservation matters, and aims to raise public awareness of protecting natural resources in rapidly growing urban areas.

The original news was published in The Tribune on 29 January 2025.
