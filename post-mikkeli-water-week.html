SansOx at Mikkeli Water Week

Everyone agrees something should be done about water quality. The recurring question at Mikkeli was who pays for it.

Mikkeli Water Week offered a programme for several different audiences. SansOx was there and presented our solutions at the Finnish Lakeland Forum and at Emerging Trends in Water Treatment. Both were interesting seminars on a topic everyone agrees on: something should be done to improve water quality.

SansOx took part with a strong team and a great many interesting discussions followed. In Finland the state of the natural waters is fairly good — but it will not stay that way unless something is done.

India raised the most questions

Our work in India, where several lakes have been rejuvenated, drew a lot of interest, and the question was usually the same: would this work with our own waters? Participants from other countries described what happens when there is no one who acts.

The solutions, such as SansOx ClariOx, exist. They just have to be put in place, and someone has to take care of the financing. The old line usually holds true in several countries: everyone agrees that someone should do something, but nobody wants to pay.
