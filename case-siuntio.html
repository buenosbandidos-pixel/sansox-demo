1The starting point

Iron and manganese are the everyday problem of Nordic groundwater: taste, colour, precipitation in the network. Conventional answer is aeration basins and filters — footprint and maintenance.

The first prototype. Garden-hose fittings and all — this unit proved separation, activation, clarification and re-dissolving in one tube. The honesty stayed; the hardware matured.

2What was installed

OxTube oxygenates the water in-line; iron oxidises and precipitates in a controlled step instead of in your network. In Siuntio the unit handled two different well chemistries at one station.
