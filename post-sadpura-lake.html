Lake project in Sadpura village, Haryana state

Kauppalehti wrote about India's water situation and SansOx's part in it. The Sadpura lake is the third restoration, carried out with a local partner.

Kauppalehti published an article on 21 March 2024, in Finnish, about the water situation in India and how SansOx is contributing to improving the quality of natural water bodies — and consequently drinking water — in the country.

As the article notes, SansOx is carrying out its third lake restoration, this time in the village of Sadpura in the state of Haryana, together with a local partner.

The same technology can be used anywhere. Even in Finland, lakes and ponds in poor condition could be restored with it.

The Kauppalehti article is behind a paywall, but a video is available showing what the lake looks like before treatment and restoration.
