Solutions

Innovative water solutions.

At SansOx, we are committed to making a positive impact across three critical areas of water management: enhancing natural water ecosystems, purifying potable water for safe consumption, and innovating in wastewater treatment. Explore our solutions below.

Natural Waters

Increasing of Dissolved Oxygen in natural water systems

All factories want to reach the situation that all water is recycled and none of it is discharged to the natural water system. Unfortunately this is not the case and in most cases big part of the treated water is discharged into the natural water systems.

Transferring lakes from carbon source to carbon sink

Lakes and ponds are usually carbon sources and big reason for this is that the water not clear but especially mud and other solids make the water “dirty”. If surface water could be clearer and sun beams could penetrate deeper to the water body, the photosynthesis could take place deeper in the water and this way more carbon could be transferred to oxygen. SansOx solutions have provided several cases where water has become more clearer in the surface of the water after being treated with OxTube and air.

Algae prevention in coastal areas

In several coastal areas even in Finland, algae is common problem. The makes the life at summer cottage miserable.

Increasing DO in fish farming

Dey land fish farming (RAS) is increasing around the world as the water bodies do not produce enough fish to the growing population. The problem often is that fish population is maximized for the amount of the water.

Golf course water hazard — GolfOx

SansOx's GolfOx helps keep the water in the water hazard in better condition. In it, air is dissolved to the water and thereby the amount of dissolved oxygen in the water is increased.

Increasing crop with drip irrigation — DripOx

Several studies show that higher DO in the irrigation water increase the growth rate of the crop. This is true with irrigation and especially with dripirrigation where water is fed to the roots of the plants in order to prevent the evaporation because of the warm weather.

Potable Water

Radon removal — RadOx

Radon is the second common cause for lung cancer. Different countries have different limits for dissolved radon in potable water.

Iron removal — IroX

Iron removal is usual water treatment when using ground water as raw water for making potable water. For this SansOx has delivered a solution called IroX that consists of OxTube, possibly GasRemox and sand filters.

Manganese removal

Manganese removal takes place often at the same time as iron removal. In manganese pH value plays more important role.

pH control — UGOx

CO2 is one way to control pH. In raw water if pH is low, then the reason is usually dissolved CO 2 .

Unwanted gas removal — UGOx

Both ground and surface water usually contain unwanted gases that should be removed before usage. UGOx is the answer to this problem.

Pharmaceutical and virus & bacteria removal — PharmOx

In cases where surface water is used as raw water to potable water, PharmOx is good solution to keep raw water quality at required level. Today most of the pharmaceutical residues go through wastewater treatment plant and end up to the natural water systems.

Process and Waste Water

Discharge water aeration — DCWOx

When waste water treatment plant discharges the water into the natural water system it usually fills the set requirements. Unfortunately DO is not one of the parameters that are followed or measured.

COD and BOD removal

Waste water quality is usually measured with COD or BOD amount and the requirements are to be lower than this amount of COD or BOD. Aeration is used to reduce COD and BOD during the treatment process.

Unwanted gas removal (wastewater)

In waste water treatment unwanted are formed and it would be good to prevent them getting into the process. UGOx is the answer to this problem.

Preventing and removal of H₂S gas

In chemical and especially pulp and paper industry forming of H 2 S is a common problem and unfortunately a lethal problem. H2S forms in unaerobic conditions and aeration is a common solution for this in order to keep the DO at certain level.
