Why Spain, and why now

Water reuse and micropollutant treatment create near-term demand for retrofit solutions. The driver is regulatory, not agronomic — the water either meets the class or it does not.

Reclaimed water

Royal Decree 1085/2024

Adopted 22 October 2024, in force since 24 October 2024. Reuse projects require defined responsibilities, monitoring and a declared point of compliance.

Funding

≥80 % producer share

Under Directive (EU) 2024/3019, a fourth treatment stage for micropollutants becomes mandatory by 2045 for plants of 150,000 population equivalent or more, with national transposition due in 2027. Producer funding covers at least 80 % of the eligible cost — the buyer is not carrying the full cost of compliance.

Retrofit

No rebuild required

Utilities need additional treatment capacity without rebuilding the full process line. OxTube installs into the existing flow.

Water security

Freshwater substitution

Reclaimed water can replace stressed freshwater resources in irrigation — where the quality class decides whether it may be used at all.

What we do not claim

Collected here, so the rest of the page can get to the point. This process is not a PFAS destruction method. A pilot result from one site is not a guarantee for another — that is what the 60–90 day pilot is for. The H₂S result from the mine pilot in northern Spain could not be verified (wind distorted the gas measurement); its oxygen result stands. The datasheet is a reading aid, not a quotation. On micropollutants there is no independent report yet — the independent results are THL’s and Savonia’s disinfection testing, and that distinction is kept visible.
