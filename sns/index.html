ENFIES

Control software · sensors · continuous water data

Treatment changes the water. Only measurement proves it.

A pump can be installed in a day. What takes longer — and what actually gets paid for — is the record showing the water is different afterwards, and that it stayed different. SpringNet builds that layer: the control software, the sensors and the continuous data behind water treatment and water-body projects.

Three things we supply

Software

SCADA and control

Supervisory control for treatment equipment: setpoints, dosing logic, alarms and remote operation. The system runs the process, not just the reporting.

Hardware

Sensors and instrumentation

Field instrumentation sized for the site and the parameters that matter, installed where the change actually happens rather than where access is easiest.

Data

Continuous data and integration

Measurement collected without gaps, stored in a form that survives an audit, and integrated with the operator's existing systems.

Why these three belong together: a sensor without control logic is a reading. Control logic without stored data is an operation nobody can verify afterwards. Sold separately they are components; sold together they are evidence.

Where it is used

Water treatment installations

Dissolved oxygen, dosing and process parameters monitored continuously so that a discharge limit or a reuse class can be demonstrated, not asserted.

Water bodies

Lakes, ponds and bays where a restoration result has to be measured against a documented baseline over years rather than sampled twice. See Smart Lake.

Crediting and compliance reporting

Where a measured change becomes a reportable or issuable unit, the monitoring record is the product. The measurement has to be built for that from the start.

What we do not do

We do not supply the treatment equipment. The oxygenation hardware comes from SansOx Oy. We are the layer that operates it and records what it did.
We do not verify our own results. Where independent attribution is required, it is provided by a third party. A measurement system that grades its own output is not evidence.

One group, one chain of evidence

A credit is only worth what the measurement behind it is worth, and a measurement is only worth what the treatment it describes actually did. These three steps are normally bought from three suppliers, and the evidence is handed over twice — each handover is where a buyer’s technical advisor starts asking questions. Here the three steps are coordinated as one delivery, not assembled afterwards.

Treat

SansOx Oy supplies the hardware in the flow: OxTube dissolving units, piping and pumps. This is the step that changes the water.

Measure

SpringNet Solutions Oy — this company — supplies the control software, the sensors and the continuous data. It records what changed, and it keeps the record.

Certify

OceanSite Oy turns a documented change into credit-ready verification material: for a carbon credit or a restoration credit.

What this chain does not yet deliver. Issuing a credit requires an approved methodology and a registry to issue it into. Neither has been selected yet; issuance will run through specialised partners once they are agreed for the buyer's market, and the record is built to align with the EU's CRCF framework in the meantime. Until then the chain delivers treatment and the evidence of it — not issued credits. This is stated here rather than discovered by the buyer in the first technical meeting.

The three companies are separately registered. The group has no published parent company or joint trading name yet, and none is claimed on this page.

Tell us what has to be proven.

The parameter, the limit and who will read the report. The instrumentation follows from that — not the other way round.
