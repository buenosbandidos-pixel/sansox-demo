Textile water · February 26, 2025

Nordic companies at the Sustainable Fashion Platform, Bangladesh

The easy improvements have already been made, the factories said. SansOx was invited back to Dhaka to look at what comes after the low-hanging fruit.

For the second time SansOx was invited to join the Sustainable Fashion Platform by Sweden event in Dhaka, Bangladesh. The event brings new technologies together to help the Bangladeshi textile industry reach better sustainability results, with H&M, Inter IKEA Group and Lindex Group as sponsors.

The participating companies sat down with manufacturers and worked through the challenges the factories face. One comment stayed with us: the low-hanging fruit has already been picked, and what the manufacturers need now are ideas from outside the box.

Inside a modern textile factory

The second day included a visit to one of the manufacturing facilities, Zaber & Zubair Fabrics Limited, where we saw how a large textile factory works and how many separate phases a modern factory has.

Water is where SansOx comes in

SansOx provides solutions for improving water quality, particularly on the wastewater side. Water recirculation is not yet at 100 %, but the target is zero liquid discharge and every factory is working towards it on its own timetable.

Thank you to the organisers, the Embassy of Sweden in Dhaka and Business Sweden, and to the sponsors H&M, IKEA and Lindex. It was a pleasure to work with you again — and thanks to all the participating factories for the discussions, several of which are still going on.
