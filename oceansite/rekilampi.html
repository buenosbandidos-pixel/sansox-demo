Rekilampi is the reference we put forward first, because someone other than us took the readings.

The site

5.2hasurface area
18mmaximum depth

A small, deep pond — the geometry where oxygen disappears near the bottom and stays gone, because depth prevents natural mixing from reaching it.

Measured change

Independently measured

MeasureBaseline2026Change
Near-bottom dissolved oxygen~0.0–0.1 mg/L 0.28–0.36 mg/L3–5×
Monitoring period2023–2026, continuous and sampled3 years
Measuring partyKarelia University of Applied Sciences — not the supplier
Read the numbers honestly. 0.28–0.36 mg/L is a large relative improvement from near-zero, and it is still a low absolute concentration. It means the deep zone is no longer anoxic. It does not yet mean the pond supports the same life it once did.

Source: Rekilampi reference letter (EN), 24 June 2026. Available on request.
