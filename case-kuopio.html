Pharmaceutical residues, removed in 0.7 seconds.

OxTube Ozone was installed in the outlet pipe of a purified municipal wastewater stream in Kuopio, Finland. One pass through the tube. No additional energy on the water side. The results were presented at IWA Ecotechnologies for Wastewater Treatment, Milan 2021.

1The starting point

Conventional treatment removes solids and nutrients — but pharmaceutical residues pass through. They end up in rivers, lakes and, eventually, the Baltic Sea. Ozone treatment works, but conventional contact basins are large, slow and energy-hungry.

Before · 0.0 s After · 1.0 s

Drag the divider. First the water churns milky as impurities are pulled apart — one second later it runs clear. Actual trial photographs from the published paper (Fig. 2), unretouched apart from cropping.

2What was installed

OxTube is a hermetic in-line device — no basin, no moving parts in the water. Four functions happen sequentially inside one tube, in under a second:

0.0 s0.7 s — treated
SeparationSoluble ingredients are separated from the water
ActivationMolecular and ionic structures are split — complex pharmaceutical molecules break
ClarificationOzone is fed straight into the nozzle zone; meeting probability of molecules is high
Re-dissolvingOxygen is dissolved back in; residual ozone is negligible

Every substance measured — including where OxTube loses

Across the total measured drug load of the Kuopio effluent, OxTube Ozone removed 86.75 % in one pass. The two conventional methods measured alongside it removed 28.83 % and 24.18 % of the same load. This was a first, unoptimised trial on rough hypothetical dosing.

Why this matters now: the updated EU Wastewater Directive introduces extended producer responsibility — the pharmaceutical and cosmetics industry must cover at least 80 % of micropollutant-removal costs. Pharmaceuticals and cosmetics account for up to 92 % of micropollutants in urban wastewater; an estimated 1.8 million kg of pharmaceutical residues reach the Baltic Sea every year. Removal is moving from voluntary to mandatory — and this trial is the measured answer. (Figures: EU Wastewater Directive; Helsingin Sanomat via SansOx, 3/2026.)

4What follows from this

The trial was run with rough, first-pass dosing — the 87–90 % reduction is a floor, not a ceiling. Because OxTube installs in the outlet pipe of an existing plant, the same setup applies to any municipal plant where pharmaceutical residues, disinfection or discharge quality are on the agenda. Overdosed ozone can be recycled directly back into the gas channel.
